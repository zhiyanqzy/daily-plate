About the estimates

Yoghurt uses the labels you provided: Chobani Vanilla 160 kcal and 13 g protein per 170 g; Zero Sugar Strawberry 70 kcal and 13 g per 170 g. Oats, chia, fruit, biscuits, coffee, rice and delivered dishes are approximate. The Lau Homecook preset assumes a 650 kcal full set and your 60% share; rice assumes 160 g dry for two and an equal split. Weigh food or use its label for better accuracy. Unknown protein is not treated as a measured zero.
